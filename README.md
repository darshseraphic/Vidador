Vidador is an ultra-minimalistic, brutalist-inspired quantified-self performance monitor. Designed for those who want ruthless efficiency over bloated gamification, Vidador is the ultimate offline telemetry dashboard for your biological hardware. 

We stripped away the noise, the ads, the loading screens, and the unnecessary social features to bring you a pure data-entry and visualization experience.

  

## CORE MODULES & FEATURES

Vidador unites four critical health pillars into a single, unified interface:

1. **Sleep Schedule (Biological Recovery):** 
   * Simple one-tap "[ ENTER SLEEP CYCLE ]" state monitoring.
   * Chronological history metrics tracking exact sleep duration down to the minute.
2. **Water Tracker (Hydration Volumetrics):** 
   * Rapid-input macros (`+250ML`, `+500ML`, `+750ML`).
   * Visual progress bar against daily target thresholds (e.g., 3000 ML).
   * Granular, timestamped consumption logs.
3. **Steps Telemetry Quantizer (Kinetic Activity):** 
   * GitHub-style heatmaps for daily and monthly activity visualization.
   * Total recount volumetrics and Maximum Step Peak (PR) tracking.
   * Computed session means for long-term trend analysis.
4. **Chrono Timing Quantizer (Meditation / Deep Work):** 
   * Minimalist execution engine timer with `+15`, `+30`, and `+60` minute quick-adds.
   * Pause/Interrupt controls for focused meditation or Pomodoro sessions.
   * Interruption logging for behavioral awareness.

*Includes Dark/Light Mode ("Toggle Visual Spectrum Mode") to match your visual preference seamlessly.*

## DATA SOVEREIGNTY: WHY LOCAL STORAGE WINS

Vidador operates entirely on a **Local Disk Sandbox Specification**. 
Unlike mainstream fitness apps that harvest your biological data to sell to advertisers, Vidador believes your data belongs to you.

* **Absolute Privacy:** Your health metrics never leave your device. No cloud sync, no servers, no data brokering.
* **Instantaneous Performance:** Because there are no server requests or API calls, the app loads and logs data at the speed of your device's local memory. Zero loading spinners.
* **Offline First (And Only):** Deep in the woods? On airplane mode? Vidador works 100% of the time, regardless of internet connectivity.
* **No Account Required:** You don't need to sign up, hand over your email, or create a password.
